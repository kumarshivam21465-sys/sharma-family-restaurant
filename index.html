Our Story

A Family Kitchen, Open to All

Mr. Ramesh Sharma and his wife Sunita opened this restaurant with one simple idea: serve guests the same food they cook for their own family.

For over two decades, every masala has been ground in-house, every dal simmered slowly, and every roti made fresh. Come as a guest, leave as family.
